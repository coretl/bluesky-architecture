# Exploration

What was tried, what was measured, and what turned out to be wrong.

Kept separate from [](architecture.md) deliberately. The architecture document
says what the design *is*; these say how it got there, which matters because a
surprising amount of it was arrived at by being wrong first. Where a conclusion
was reversed, the reasoning that led to it is usually still worth reading — that
is why none of this is edited away.
